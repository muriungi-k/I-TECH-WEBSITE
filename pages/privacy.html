Privacy & cookies

We use the details you choose to share to respond to enquiries about our technology services.

Contact enquiries

The enquiry form prepares a draft in your email app. It does not send or store your details on this website. Your email provider handles the message when you send it. Please avoid including passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Your cookie choices

We store your consent choice in a first-party cookie for 180 days. This cookie contains only your preference and its date, never your contact details. We also keep a fallback copy in local browser storage, where available.

Optional external fonts are disabled by default. If you allow them, your browser contacts Google Fonts to download the Inter typeface, sharing connection details such as your IP address with Google. Rejecting them keeps a system font and all site features working.

This website currently uses no advertising or analytics trackers. You can change your choice at any time using the button below. Withdrawing consent reloads the page to stop external font requests.

External services

Phone, email, WhatsApp and map links open services outside this website. Their providers handle information according to their own privacy policies.
